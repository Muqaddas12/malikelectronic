1. INTRODUCTION
---------------
MaliK Electronic ("we", "our", or "us") provides the MaliK Electronic mobile 
application (the "App") designed as a technical guide and utility companion 
for electronics technicians, engineers, and inverter repair professionals.

This Privacy Policy informs you about how our App handles information, what 
data is accessed, and your rights when using the App.

By installing and using the MaliK Electronic App, you agree to the practices 
described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please 
uninstall the App and discontinue its use.


2. WHAT THE APP DOES
--------------------
MaliK Electronic is a dedicated reference and diagnostic utility providing:
  • Inverter fault diagnosis and troubleshooting guides (Luminous, Microtek, 
    Su-Kam, Livguard, and related models)
  • Schematic circuit diagrams and PCB layout inspection with pinch-to-zoom
  • Microcontroller pinout details and voltage reference guides
  • SMD resistor code decoder (3-Digit, 4-Digit, EIA-96, R/K/M notation)
  • Through-hole DIP resistor color code calculator (4-Band & 5-Band)
  • IC Pinout & Working Principles directory (200+ power & inverter ICs)
  • Bilingual interface support (English & Hindi)
  • Quick technical support contact shortcuts

4. INFORMATION STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE
---------------------------------------------
The App stores only minimal user interface preferences locally on your device:

  • Selected Appearance: Light, Dark, or System default theme

This preference data is stored exclusively inside the application's private,

to any external server, developer, or third party. Uninstalling the application 
permanently deletes all locally stored preferences automatically.


5. INTERNET USAGE & DIAGRAM STREAMING
--------------------------------------
The App requests the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission solely for the 
following functionality:

  • Cloud Diagram Viewing:
    To keep the app download size lightweight and efficient, high-resolution 
    circuit diagrams are securely streamed on-demand over encrypted HTTPS 
    from cloud content delivery servers (Google Drive CDN).
  • Security & Obfuscation:
    All cloud diagram references are protected using an in-app native 
    cryptographic cipher and decrypted only in-memory during viewing.
  • No Personal Data Sent:
    When the App loads a circuit diagram, it performs a standard HTTPS GET 
    request for the image asset only. No user identifiers, cookies, or 
    tracking parameters are sent or logged.


6. PERMISSIONS REQUESTED
-------------------------
The App requests only the permissions strictly required to operate:

  • INTERNET (`android.permission.INTERNET`):
    Required to fetch high-resolution schematic diagrams on demand.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):
    Used by the operating system network stack to detect internet availability 
    when fetching diagrams.

No sensitive or dangerous Android permissions (such as CAMERA, READ_CONTACTS, 
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, or RECORD_AUDIO) are requested 
or utilized.
