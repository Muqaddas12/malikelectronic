MaliK Electronic is an educational and reference guide application developed to assist electronics technicians, engineers, and repair specialists in troubleshooting and repairing inverters, power supplies, and printed circuit boards (PCBs).

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information is used by the App and how it is protected. By installing or using this App, you accept the terms of this Privacy Policy.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a personalized user experience, the App saves minimal preferences locally inside your device's isolated application sandbox (via AsyncStorage):

  • ✓ Language Preference: English or Hindi.
  • ✓ Theme Preference: Light, Dark, or System mode.

This data remains strictly on your device and is never sent to any external server or third party. Uninstalling the app automatically and permanently removes all stored preferences.

4. Internet Usage & Diagram Delivery

The App requires the android.permission.INTERNET permission exclusively for the following purpose:

  • High-Resolution Schematic Diagrams: To maintain a small app download size, circuit diagrams are securely retrieved over HTTPS from cloud content delivery infrastructure (Google Drive CDN).
  • Cryptographic Protection: Cloud diagram reference paths are encrypted within the app using a native cryptographic cipher and decrypted only in-memory during viewing.
  • Standard HTTPS Requests: Only standard GET requests for images are made. No user telemetry, account tokens, or advertising cookies are sent.

5. Permissions Requested

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Used strictly to stream schematic circuit diagrams over secure HTTPS.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used by the OS network stack to verify connectivity before requesting images.

No dangerous or intrusive permissions are requested or used.

6. Advertising and Analytics
