1. INTRODUCTION
---------------
MaliK Electronic ("we", "our", or "us") operates the MaliK Electronic mobile
application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App
collects, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

By downloading or using the App you agree to the practices described in this
Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App and stop using it.


2. WHAT THIS APP DOES
---------------------
MaliK Electronic is an offline inverter repair & troubleshooting guide for
electronics technicians. Its features include:

  • Inverter fault diagnosis and step-by-step repair guides
    (Luminous, Microtek, Su-Kam, Livguard and other brands)
  • Circuit diagrams and PCB photo viewer with pinch-to-zoom
  • Microcontroller pin-detail PDF viewer
  • SMD & DIP resistor calculators
  • IC Pinout & Working Guide (200+ ICs)
  • Bilingual interface (English / Hindi)
  • Contact information for MaliK Electronic shop

The App is designed to work entirely OFFLINE. No account creation or login
is required.


3. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT
---------------------------------
We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any of the following:

  ✗ Personal identifiable information (name, email, phone number, address)
  ✗ Location data (GPS or network-based)
  ✗ Camera or microphone access
  ✗ Contacts, call logs, or SMS
  ✗ Photos or files from your device storage
  ✗ Device identifiers (IMEI, Advertising ID, etc.)
  ✗ Usage analytics or crash reports sent to any remote server
  ✗ Browsing history or clipboard contents
  ✗ Financial or payment information

The App does NOT connect to any external server or API during normal use.
All content (fault guides, circuit diagrams, IC data, resistor tables) is
bundled inside the App itself and loaded locally.


4. INFORMATION STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE
---------------------------------------------
The App saves the following data ONLY on your device (never sent anywhere):

  • Your language preference (English or Hindi)
  • Your appearance preference (Light / Dark / System theme)

This data is stored using React Native's AsyncStorage, which is private to
the App on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes this data automatically.
